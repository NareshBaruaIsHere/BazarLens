BAZARLENS is a Daily Kacha Bazar Price Tracking Platform

Project Description
BazarLens is a crowd-sourced market price tracking platform designed to help people monitor and compare the prices of everyday products in local markets in Bangladesh.
The system collects current prices of products such as rice, vegetables, fish, meat, and other essential goods. It provides average, lowest, and highest prices for different areas and also shows historical price trends and market comparisons.

User Roles
1. General User
- Signs in to the system.
- Provides their city and thana.
- Views products and prices based on their location.
- Can monitor local market prices and trends.
- Can Submits current market prices.

2. Agent
- Submits current market prices.
- Every submitted price is authenticated and validated.
- Normal prices are automatically approved.
- Unusual prices are flagged for Admin review.

3. Admin
- Assign & manage Agents & User 
- Reviews flagged price reports.
- Checks the submitted evidence.
- Approves or rejects unusual price reports.
- Manages the reliability of submitted market data.

Main Features
- Location-based product and price information
- Market price submission
- Price validation
- Unusual price detection
- Admin review and approval
- Average, lowest, and highest price calculation
- Historical price trends
- Market comparison
- Public price statistics
- Data visualization

Data Storage
Approved market price data is stored in PostgreSQL and is used to generate public statistics, price trends, and other market information.

Project Goal
The main goal of BazarLens is to make local market prices more transparent and help consumers understand and compare daily market prices in Bangladesh.
